Adil's Letter
Meera,

If you’re reading this, it means I couldn’t come back. I’m sorry I couldn’t say this to you in person—I always imagined standing beside you, your voice rising, my drums answering. But some songs end too soon. I wasn’t afraid of dying. I was afraid of leaving things unfinished—Aarav, my music… and you.

Then you came into my life like rain after a long drought. Your voice didn’t just fill silence—it healed it. For the first time since Aarav was gone, music felt like forgiveness, not pain.

So promise me this—

Don’t choose silence.
Sing, even when it hurts.
And when you sing our song, don’t make it a goodbye. Sing it like we’re still there, together.

If you ever hear distant drums in the rain, don’t be afraid. It’s just me… keeping rhythm for you.

And Meera— Loving you was the bravest thing I ever did.

So live loudly. Love deeply. And when the rain falls, know my heartbeat still echoes your name.

Always yours,
Adil ❤️
